Method

How this decides

One unit, every time

A unit is 1% of the bankroll and every qualifying bet is the same size. A bigger edge does not buy a bigger bet, because the edge estimate is exactly what is least certain.

A bet needs a positive edge, not a likely winner

The bar is the probability the price demands, vig included. A -300 favourite demands 75%: calling it 70% is a five-point NEGATIVE edge on a bet that still wins most of the time.

The probability comes from the market, not from us

Measured on 854 held-out games, the de-vigged market beats this repo's own model (log loss 0.6073 vs 0.6395) and a fitted blend put 0% weight on the model. So fair value is built from OTHER books than the one being bet — the edge hunted is price dispersion, not opinion.

Nothing to bet is the normal night

Measured across a full NFL board priced against Pinnacle: 16 of 670 quotes were +EV at all and none cleared 2%. A scan that finds nothing is the system working.

The unit moves once a month, on the bankroll, never on a feeling

Up after a winning month, held after a losing one. Three losses in a row does not change tomorrow's unit — that reflex is what empties a small bankroll, not the losses.

Everything is scored, bet or not

Every call is logged and graded on closing-line value first. CLV resolves in days on every prediction; win rate needs hundreds of bets before it separates skill from luck.

The rules, short

  • One unit per bet. Not two because you love it.
  • A bet needs a positive gap, not a big favourite. High probability is not an edge.
  • If nothing clears the bar, bet nothing. A no-bet day is a normal day.
  • Three losses in a row does not change tomorrow's unit.
  • The unit moves once a month, on the bankroll, never on a feeling.
  • Write the reason and the biggest risk before the result is known.
  • No parlays as strategy. Correlated legs are one bet, not several.
  • Don't chase a price that has already moved.
